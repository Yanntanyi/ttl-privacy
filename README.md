## Privacy

### App Privacy questionnaire

What TTL actually collects:

| Data type | Collected | Linked to user | Used for | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Email address | Yes | Yes | App Functionality | No |
| Name | Yes | Yes | App Functionality | No |
| User Content (other) | Yes | Yes | App Functionality | No |
| User ID | Yes | Yes | App Functionality | No |

Answer **No** to "Do you use data to track?" — you have no ads, no analytics, no
third-party SDK beyond Firebase Auth and Firestore, and no cross-app tracking.

"User Content (other)" covers the entry text, notes, and honor citations.

### Privacy policy draft

Host at a public URL. GitHub Pages: make a repo, add this as `index.md`, enable
Pages in Settings, use the resulting URL.

```
PRIVACY POLICY FOR TTL

Last updated: August 30, 2026

TTL is a private score-tracking app for small groups. This policy explains what it stores and why.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Account information. When you create an account we store your email address and the display name you choose. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). We never see or store your password.

Content you create. Entries you log, notes you write, honors you award yourself, and the league you belong to. This is the app's purpose — without it there is nothing to show.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, health data, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. We do not use cookies or trackers. There is no advertising in TTL.

HOW IT IS USED

Only to run the app: signing you in, showing your leaderboards, and displaying your league's activity to other members of that league. Nothing else.

WHO CAN SEE IT

Only other members of your league, which is joined by a private invite code shared directly by an existing member. Nothing in TTL is public. There is no discovery, no search, and no way for anyone outside your league to see your content.

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers. Google processes it on our behalf as our infrastructure provider and does not use it for its own purposes. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else, ever.

DELETING YOUR DATA

You can delete any entry or honor you created at any time from inside the app.

You can delete your entire account from the You tab. This permanently removes your account, your profile, and your entries. This cannot be undone.

You can also email Smartyt8@me.com and we will delete your account within 30 days.

CHILDREN

TTL is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

CHANGES

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.

CONTACT

Smartyt8@me.com
```
